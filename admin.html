Text dashboard

Every piece of text on the RFD4 visualizer pages is here, grouped by page. Edit a box and the preview below it updates. Press Save to write the changes to data/text.json. The old file is copied to data/backups/ first, so nothing is lost. Press Publish to commit the saved text and push it.

Words in curly brackets, like {stage}, are filled in by the page. Keep them in the text. HTML tags such as <code> and links work. Use Show on page to open the page with every editable piece of text outlined (hover over one to see its name). The real RFD4 output on the pages is not editable here, and neither are the numbers from the captured runs.
